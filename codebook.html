General rules — read before coding

The category is decided on the proper-name element only, not the generic word.

(a) Generic words (道 / 街 / 里 / 徑 / 臺 / 坊 → Road / Street / Lane / Path / Terrace) are excluded from the category and recorded separately (§4.5).
(b) Directional suffixes (東 / 西 / 中 / 南 / 北) and section suffixes (－X 段) are excluded from the category and recorded in notes. In the population, 177 streets have a directional suffix and 33 have a section suffix.
(c) Historical direction does not affect the category: a colonial-era English name later rendered in Chinese (彌敦道 Nathan Road) and a Chinese name later romanised in English (龍翔道 Lung Cheung Road) are both transliteration. Judge the correspondence, not the history.
(d) When in doubt, check the decision rule first. If still in doubt, record the entry as a boundary case. Do not guess.

4.1 音譯 Transliteration

Sound-based rendering of the proper name.

Definition (draft): 專名部分以聲音對應：中文名是英文名的音譯，或英文名是中文名的粵語拼音／慣用羅馬拼音。

Theory: Vinay & Darbelnet — borrowing (借用); Venuti (1995) — foreignization (異化), preserving the source-language sound.

Decision rule: 如英文專名部分的讀音與中文專名部分的粵語讀音對應，或中文是英文名的音譯，即為音譯。不看意思。

Positive: 遮打道 Chater Road (遮打 = Chater 音譯); 龍翔道 Lung Cheung Road (Lung Cheung = 龍翔粵語拼音).

Negative: 花園道 Garden Road — Garden 是花園的意思，並非聲音對應，因此不是音譯。

Boundary case: 廣東道 Canton Road — Canton 歷史上由葡文 Cantão 音譯而來，現為慣用 exonym。決定：音譯（形式上為聲音對應），notes 記錄歷史來源。

4.2 直譯 Literal translation

Word-for-word meaning-based rendering.

Definition (draft): 專名部分逐字按意思對譯，每個實義成分均有對應的英文詞。

Theory: Vinay & Darbelnet — literal translation / calque (仿譯).

Decision rule: 如中文專名部分的每個實義成分均可逐字對應英文詞，即為直譯。

Positive: 花園道 Garden Road (花園 = garden); 牡丹徑 Peony Path (牡丹 = peony).

Negative: 彌敦道 Nathan Road — Nathan 並非彌敦的字面意思，因此不是直譯。

Boundary case: 山頂道 Peak Road — 山頂逐字對應 peak，字面對應成立，決定為直譯；但 Peak 同時是山頂的既定英文名，與意譯有所重疊，notes 記錄。

4.3 意譯 Free translation

An established or functionally equivalent English name.

Definition (draft): 專名部分採用既定或功能上對等的英文名，既非逐字對譯，亦非聲音對應。

Theory: Vinay & Darbelnet — equivalence (等值); Venuti (1995) — domestication (歸化).

Decision rule: 如英文專名與中文專名在意思上對等，或為該地方／人物的既定英文名，但既非逐字翻譯、亦非拼音，即為意譯。

Positive: 皇后大道東 Queen's Road East（皇后 → Queen's，意思對等，並非逐字 “Empress Road”）；英皇道 King's Road（英皇 → King's，既定對等）。

Negative: 龍翔道 Lung Cheung Road — 聲音對應，並非意思對等，因此不是意譯。

Boundary case: 金鐘道 Queensway — 金鐘 (golden bell) 與 Queensway 既無聲音關係、亦無語義關係，英文名為更名／既定名。決定：意譯（既定名），notes 標明 boundary；如 full 500 中此類超過約 10 條，考慮加註或分開記錄。

4.4 混合 Hybrid

Sound and meaning in the same proper name.

Definition (draft): 專名部分同時包含聲音成分與意思成分。

Theory: Vinay & Darbelnet — combined procedures (音譯加意譯); Newmark — proper names 混合處理.

Decision rule: 如專名部分一部分為聲音對應、一部分為意思對譯，即為混合。

Positive: 利園山道 Lee Garden Road (利 = Lee 音譯 + 園 = Garden 意譯); 粉嶺車站路 Fanling Station Road (粉嶺 = Fanling 音譯 + 車站 = Station 意譯).

Negative: 遮打道 Chater Road — 專名部分只有聲音成分 Chater，並無意思成分，因此不是混合。

Boundary case: 青山公路－洪水橋段 Castle Peak Road - Hung Shui Kiu — 青山 → Castle Peak（意譯）+ 洪水橋 → Hung Shui Kiu（音譯），再加分段詞尾。決定：混合，按主路名分類，分段記入 notes。

4.5 Generic words (secondary — not a category)

Generic words are almost always translated by dictionary equivalence: 道 → Road, 街 → Street, 里 → Lane, 徑 → Path, 臺 → Terrace. Exceptions are recorded in notes and may be used for secondary analysis (for example, a generic-word correspondence table or heatmap).

街 → Crescent：多實街 Dorset Crescent（按形狀，並非 Street）。

坊 → Fong：丁貴坊街 Ting Kwai Fong Street (保留音譯)。

Generic words do not affect the four categories above; the category is decided on the proper-name element only.

Pilot test (30 entries, 19 November 2026)

As the brief requires, the codebook was tested on 20–30 entries before the full 500 were coded. Distribution in the pilot (first 30 entries of the sample): Transliteration 23, Literal 2, Free 0, Hybrid 5.

Finding 1: Free translation was 0 in the pilot, so it was expected to be the smallest category in the full 500. The category was kept (Queen's Road East and similar major roads are Free), and the final count was watched.

Finding 2: directional suffixes (177 streets) and section suffixes (33 streets) are common, so general rule (b) was added: suffixes are excluded from the category and recorded in notes.

Finding 3: generic-word exceptions (街 → Crescent) led to §4.5, recording generics separately.
